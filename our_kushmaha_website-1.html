About Our Kushmaha

Our Kushmaha is a village-focused digital platform created to share local news, village updates, events, culture, traditions, achievements, photos and videos related to Kushmaha, Banka, Bihar.

Our aim is to connect people of Kushmaha and showcase the village and its community to a wider audience through digital media.

Founder — Ishu Kumar

Ishu Kumar is the founder of Our Kushmaha. He works to digitally showcase Kushmaha village and share its stories, events, culture and community updates.

Ishu Kumar is also a skilled drone pilot, creating aerial photography and videography to capture the landscapes, events and beautiful views of Kushmaha.
